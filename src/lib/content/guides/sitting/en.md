# Sitting volleyball explained

Sitting volleyball is a Paralympic sport played by six players on each side, sitting on the floor. Most of the game is indoor volleyball: rotation, the overlap rule, three hits, the libero and rally scoring work as they do on this site. The differences come from playing seated. The rules are World ParaVolley's [Official Sitting Volleyball Rules 2025–2028](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf).

<Callout>

**In short:** a 10 × 6 m court and a net about 1.1 m high; players judged by where their **buttocks** are, not their feet; you must stay seated when playing the ball; and you **may block the serve**.

</Callout>

## Court and net

|                  | Sitting volleyball               | Indoor (FIVB)                 |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Court            | 10 × 6 m (each half 5 × 6 m)     | 18 × 9 m                      |
| Attack line      | 2 m from the centre line         | 3 m                           |
| Net height       | 1.15 m men, 1.05 m women         | 2.43 m men, 2.24 m women      |
| Net and antennas | Net 0.80 m deep; antennas 1.60 m | Net 1 m deep; antennas 1.80 m |
| Service zone     | 6 m wide                         | 9 m wide                      |

See [rules 1 and 2 of the sitting rules](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=15).

## The seat rule

- When you play the ball, some part of your body between the buttocks and the shoulders must touch the floor. Losing that contact is a **lifting** fault ([rule 9.3.5](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=29)).
- In a defensive action you may lift briefly if the ball is not entirely above the top of the net. A short loss of contact is never allowed when attacking, and standing up, raising the body or taking steps is always a fault ([rule 9.4](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=30)).
- Positions in the rotation, the attack line and the service rules are all judged by your **buttocks**, not your feet. A back-row attacker's buttocks must not touch or cross the attack line at the hit ([rule 13.2.2](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=34)).

## Serving and blocking

- At the service hit the server's buttocks must be in the service zone, not on the court or end line, though legs and hands may be inside the court ([rule 12.4.3](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=33)).
- **Blocking the serve is allowed** ([rule 14.5](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=35)), unlike in indoor volleyball.
- Lifting while blocking is a fault only if you touch the ball ([rule 14.6.3](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=35)).
- Under the net, any part of the body may touch the opponents' court as long as it does not interfere ([rule 11.2.2](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=31)).

## Rotation, libero, substitutions and sets

As in indoor volleyball under FIVB rules: the receiving team must be in rotational order at the serve and the serving team may stand anywhere ([rule 7.4](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=26)); up to two liberos, who may not serve or block; six substitutions and two time-outs per set; sets to 25 and a deciding fifth set to 15.

## Player classes

Players are classified by how much their impairment affects them in the sport: **VS1** (more affected) and **VS2** (minimally affected). A team may have at most two VS2 players on its roster and **at most one VS2 player on court** at a time ([rules 4.1.1 and 7.3.1](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025-2028-WPV-Official-Sitting-Volleyball-Rules-English-v1-2025-03-18.pdf#page=20); [classification rules](https://worldparavolley.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/06182026-WPV-Classification-Rules-FINAL.pdf)).

## Where to play

- **England:** Volleyball England runs sitting volleyball, including the Grand Prix series ([sitting volleyball at Volleyball England](https://volleyballengland.org/get-involved/play/sitting-volleyball)).
- **Hungary:** news of the sitting volleyball national teams is on the [MRSZ site](https://hunvolley.hu/index.php/category/uloroplabda/) and at the [Hungarian Paralympic Committee](https://hparalimpia.hu/sportag/uloroplabda).
