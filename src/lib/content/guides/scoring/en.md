# How to score a volleyball match

Every club needs volunteers at the scorer's table. The scorer keeps the official record of the match and makes sure the right player serves. This guide follows the FIVB rules: the scorer's duties are [rule 27](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=58) and the assistant scorer's [rule 28](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=59). Your competition may use its own scoresheet, so ask for its instructions too.

<Callout>

**The one thing that matters most:** check that the right player serves. If the wrong player serves, report it straight after the service hit, by sounding the buzzer.

</Callout>

## Before the match and each set

- Fill in the match and team details, with each libero's name and number on the libero lines ([rule 27.2.1](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=58)).
- Get the captains' and coaches' signatures.
- Copy each team's starting line-up from its line-up sheet. Check the numbers with the second referee before confirming them ([FIVB guidelines](https://inside.cev.eu/media/bm4jik3r/new-guidelines-instructions-2025.pdf#page=7)). If a team's line-up sheet is late, tell the second referee at once.
- The assistant scorer prepares the libero control sheet and a spare scoresheet ([rule 28.2.1](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=59)).

The [line-up page](/lineup) on this site shows what to write on the line-up slip: positions I to VI are zones 1 to 6.

## During each rally

- **Points:** record every point as it is won.
- **Serving order:** check that the right player serves. A wrong server is a rotational fault: the opponents get a point and the serve, and if you can tell when the fault began, the points the faulty team scored since then are cancelled ([rule 7.7](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=28)).
- **Substitutions:** a request starts when the substitute enters the substitution zone. Acknowledge it with the buzzer, check it is allowed (FIVB allows six per team per set, and each starter may leave and come back once), record it and tell the second referee ([rules 15 and 27.2.2.3](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=38)). With several at once, record them one pair at a time.
- **Time-outs:** record each one; FIVB allows two per team per set, of 30 seconds ([rule 15.4.1](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=39)). Report a request the rules do not allow.
- **Libero replacements:** the assistant scorer records every one on the libero control sheet. They are not substitutions, but there must be a completed rally between two of them ([rule 19.3.2](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=45)); report an illegal replacement immediately.
- **Sanctions:** record warnings, penalties and delay sanctions when the second referee tells you to.
- Announce the end of each set, and the 8th point in the deciding set, when teams change ends ([rule 18.2.2](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf)).

## At the end of the match

- Record the final result.
- If a team protests, a statement is written only with the first referee's permission.
- Sign the scoresheet first, then the captains, then the referees ([rule 27.2.3](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=59)).
