# Playing libero

The libero is a back-row specialist in a different shirt who passes and digs. You come on for any back-row player without it counting as a substitution, most often for the middle blockers, and you take the biggest share of serve receive. The [libero guide](/guides/libero) covers the rules in full.

## Every moment of the rally

- **Serve receive:** you are the main passer, usually in the middle of the passing line where most serves go. Call the ball early and take everything you can.
- **When your middle serves:** under FIVB rules you may not serve, so the middle you replace comes back on to serve, and you wait off court until your team loses the serve. Some leagues let the libero serve in one position; see [rules by league](/guides/leagues).
- **Defense:** dig. In this site's tools you defend left back (zone 5) unless your team plays you in middle back.
- **Attack:** you never attack. Cover your hitters, and when the setter takes the first ball, you are usually the one who sets.

## Rules that matter most to you

- You may not serve (FIVB), block or try to block, or attack a ball that is entirely above the top of the net from anywhere ([rule 19.3.1](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=44)).
- If you set with your fingers from in front of the attack line, your hitter may not attack that ball while it is entirely above the net ([rule 19.3.1.4](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=45)). Set from behind the line, or with a forearm pass.
- There must be a completed rally between two of your replacements ([rule 19.3.2](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=45)).

## Common mistakes

- Setting with your fingers from in front of the 3 m line when the hitter then attacks above the net.
- Coming on or off at the wrong place: use the replacement zone between the attack line and the end line by your bench.
- Waiting for the ball instead of moving to it early.
- Forgetting that you are off court when your middle serves, and walking on too soon.
