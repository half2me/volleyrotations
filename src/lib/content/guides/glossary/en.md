# Volleyball glossary, English and Hungarian

<Callout>

**In short**

- Terms are grouped by topic: positions, court, skills, tactics and rules.
- Each row gives the English term, the usual Hungarian term and a short explanation.
- Hungarian coaches often use the English word too (libero, pipe, stack). Where a term has two common Hungarian forms, both are listed.
- Use your browser's find (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F) to jump to a word.

</Callout>

## Positions and roles

| English                   | Hungarian                      | Explanation                                                                                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Setter (S)                | feladó                         | Takes the second touch and sets up the attack.                                                |
| Outside hitter (OH)       | nyitásfogadó (4-es) ütő        | Passes and attacks from the left side.                                                        |
| Middle blocker (MB)       | center                         | Blocks in the middle and hits quick sets.                                                     |
| Opposite (OPP)            | feladó átló, átlóütő, 2-es ütő | Attacks from the right, stands opposite the setter in the rotation.                           |
| Libero (L)                | liberó                         | Back-row specialist in a different shirt; cannot serve (FIVB), block or attack above the net. |
| Defensive specialist (DS) | védekező specialista           | Substitute who comes on to pass and dig in the back row.                                      |
| Server                    | nyitó                          | The player in zone 1 who serves.                                                              |
| Front-row player          | első sorköteles játékos        | A player in zone 4, 3 or 2 at the serve.                                                      |
| Back-row player           | hátsó sorköteles játékos       | A player in zone 5, 6 or 1 at the serve.                                                      |
| Captain                   | csapatkapitány, játékkapitány  | Speaks to the referees for the team.                                                          |
| Coach                     | edző                           | Gives the line-up, asks for substitutions and time-outs.                                      |
| First referee             | első játékvezető               | Stands on the referee stand at the net and runs the match.                                    |
| Second referee            | második játékvezető            | Stands on the floor opposite the first referee; watches the net and the centre line.          |
| Scorer                    | jegyzőkönyvvezető              | Keeps the score sheet and the rotation order.                                                 |

## The court

| English                 | Hungarian                  | Explanation                                                               |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Court                   | pálya                      | 18 × 9 m, each half 9 × 9 m.                                              |
| Zone 1 to 6             | 1–6. hely                  | The six positions at the serve: front 4, 3, 2; back 5, 6, 1.              |
| Front zone              | első zóna, támadózóna      | Between the centre line and the attack line.                              |
| Back zone               | hátsó zóna                 | Between the attack line and the end line.                                 |
| Attack line, 3 m line   | támadóvonal, hármas vonal  | 3 m from the centre line; limits back-row attacks.                        |
| End line                | alapvonal                  | The back line of the court.                                               |
| Sideline                | oldalvonal                 | The long lines on each side.                                              |
| Centre line             | középvonal                 | The line under the net dividing the two courts.                           |
| Free zone               | kifutó                     | The area around the court where play is still allowed.                    |
| Service zone            | nyitási zóna               | Behind the end line, between the sideline extensions.                     |
| Libero replacement zone | Libero helyettesítési zóna | Part of the free zone by the bench, between the attack line and end line. |
| Net                     | háló                       | 2.43 m for men, 2.24 m for women.                                         |
| Antenna                 | antenna                    | Rods on the net marking the crossing space.                               |
| Target                  | feladóhely                 | Where the pass should go: just right of the middle, near the net.         |

## Skills

| English             | Hungarian                 | Explanation                                                     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Serve               | nyitás (adogatás)         | Puts the ball into play from behind the end line.               |
| Float serve         | lebegtetett nyitás        | A serve with no spin that moves unpredictably.                  |
| Jump serve          | felugrós nyitás           | A serve hit in the air, often with topspin.                     |
| Ace                 | ász                       | A serve that wins the point directly.                           |
| Serve receive, pass | fogadás, nyitásfogadás    | The first contact on the opponent's serve.                      |
| Forearm pass, bump  | alkarérintés              | Passing with the forearms held together.                        |
| Overhand pass       | kosárérintés              | Playing the ball with the fingers above the head.               |
| Set                 | feladás                   | The second touch, setting up the attack.                        |
| Attack, spike       | támadás, leütés           | Sending the ball over with a hit.                               |
| Quick attack        | gyors, gyorsindítás       | A fast, low set to the middle blocker.                          |
| Pipe                | pipe, hátsó közép támadás | A back-row attack from the middle, from behind the attack line. |
| Slide               | csúsztatás                | The middle blocker's one-foot attack behind the setter.         |
| Tip                 | ejtés                     | A soft touch over or around the block.                          |
| Roll shot           | lassított ütés            | A slower, looping attack.                                       |
| Block               | sánc                      | Front-row players reaching above the net to stop the attack.    |
| Dig                 | mezőnyvédekezés, védés    | Keeping an attack off the floor.                                |
| Free ball           | ajándéklabda              | An easy ball sent over without an attack.                       |
| Down ball           | állóütés                  | A hit over the net without jumping, easier to defend.           |

## Tactics

| English            | Hungarian                | Explanation                                                           |
| ------------------ | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rotation           | forgás (rotáció)         | Moving one place clockwise when your team wins back the serve.        |
| Rotation 1 to 6    | 1–6. forgás              | The six line-ups, named after where the setter stands.                |
| Sideout            | nyitásjog visszaszerzése | Winning a rally on the opponent's serve.                              |
| Switch             | posztváltás              | Moving to your specialty spot after the serve.                        |
| Stack, hide        | rejtés                   | Placing players legally so they are near their spot before the serve. |
| 5-1 system         | 5:1-es rendszer          | Five hitters, one setter.                                             |
| 6-2 system         | 6:2-es rendszer          | Two setters, the back-row one sets.                                   |
| 4-2 system         | 4:2-es rendszer          | Two setters, the front-row one sets.                                  |
| 6-6 system         | 6:6-os rendszer          | Everyone sets in turn.                                                |
| Transition         | átmenet                  | Moving from defense to attack, or back.                               |
| Perimeter defense  | hátsós (6-os hátul)      | Middle back stays deep.                                               |
| Middle-up defense  | sarkos (6-os elöl)       | Middle back moves up behind the block for tips.                       |
| Rotational defense | forgó védekezés          | The back row rotates towards the attack.                              |
| Attack coverage    | biztosítás               | Teammates crouching around the hitter to save a blocked ball.         |
| Off-blocker        | sáncon kívüli játékos    | The front-row player not in the block.                                |
| Seam               | rés                      | The gap between two passers or defenders.                             |
| Line               | egyenes                  | An attack down the sideline.                                          |
| Cross, angle       | átló                     | An attack diagonally across the court.                                |

## Rules

| English                   | Hungarian                      | Explanation                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rally                     | labdamenet                     | From the serve until the ball is out of play.                                                             |
| Rally scoring             | minden labdamenet pontot ér    | Every rally scores a point.                                                                               |
| Set                       | játszma                        | Played to 25 (fifth set 15), win by 2.                                                                    |
| Match                     | mérkőzés                       | Best of five sets in FIVB play.                                                                           |
| Starting line-up          | kezdő állásrend                | The order of the six starters, handed in before each set.                                                 |
| Overlap, positional fault | állásrendi hiba, átfedés       | Receiving team out of rotation order at the serve.                                                        |
| Rotational fault          | forgásrendi hiba               | Serving out of order.                                                                                     |
| Substitution              | csere                          | Replacing a player; 6 per set.                                                                            |
| Exceptional substitution  | kivételes játékoscsere         | Replacing an injured player when a normal substitution is not possible.                                   |
| Libero replacement        | liberó-helyettesítés           | The libero coming on or off; not a substitution.                                                          |
| Time-out                  | pihenőidő                      | 30-second break; 2 per team per set.                                                                      |
| Double contact            | kettős érintés                 | One player touching the ball twice in a row; a fault, with exceptions on the first contact and the block. |
| Catch, lift               | fogott labda                   | Holding or throwing the ball instead of hitting it.                                                       |
| Four hits                 | négy érintés                   | Playing the ball more than three times; the block does not count.                                         |
| Net fault                 | hálóérintés                    | Touching the net between the antennae while playing the ball.                                             |
| Centre line fault         | középvonal-átlépés             | Crossing the centre line in a way the rules forbid.                                                       |
| Foot fault                | lépéshiba                      | Stepping on the court or end line when serving.                                                           |
| Back-row attack fault     | hátsó sorköteles támadási hiba | A back-row player attacking above the net from in front of the attack line.                               |
| In, out                   | bent, kint                     | Whether the ball lands inside the lines; the lines are in.                                                |

The full rules: [FIVB Official Volleyball Rules 2025–2028](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf).
