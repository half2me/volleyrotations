# Rules by league

Most of indoor volleyball is the same everywhere: six players, rotational order, three contacts, rally scoring. A few rules that change what you see on a rotation diagram differ between rule sets. Pick your league in the [explorer](/explorer), the [overlap trainer](/overlap), the [line-up page](/lineup) or the [match tracker](/match), and the tools follow it.

<Callout>

**Check your own competition.** Leagues can change these rules in their own regulations, and rulebooks change between seasons. The table reflects the rulebooks in force in November 2026.

</Callout>

## The serving team's positions

Since 2025 the FIVB checks rotational order for the receiving team only: the serving team may stand anywhere on its own court at the serve ([FIVB rule 7.4](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=27)). The other rule sets have kept the older rule, where both teams must be in order and only the server is exempt: [NCAA rule 10.3.1](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/rules/women/PRWVB_RulesBook.pdf#page=48) and [USA Volleyball rule 7.4](https://usavolleyball.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2025-2027-USAV-Indoor-Rules-Book_FINAL.pdf#page=37). The NFHS rulebook is not published openly; state-association rulings for the 2025–26 and 2026–27 seasons still judge overlap on the serving team.

What this means on court: under FIVB rules your team can wait in its own spots while you serve; elsewhere, everyone except the server switches at the serve hit, just as they do on the opponent's serve. The [overlap guide](/guides/overlap) explains the rule itself.

## The libero serving

Under FIVB rules the libero may not serve ([rule 19.3.1.3](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=44)), so the middle the libero replaces comes back on to serve. NCAA ([rule 12.1.2.3](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/rules/women/PRWVB_RulesBook.pdf#page=56)), NFHS and USA Volleyball ([rule 19.3.1.3b](https://usavolleyball.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2025-2027-USAV-Indoor-Rules-Book_FINAL.pdf#page=61)) let the libero serve in **one** position of the service order. Most teams use it for one of the middles, so that middle never has to come on just to serve. With two liberos, NCAA and, from 2026–27, NFHS still allow only that one serving position ([NFHS 2026–27 rule changes](https://demo.osaa.org/docs/vbl/2026NFHSrulechanges.pdf)).

In the tools, choose which middle the libero serves for under the team settings.

## Substitutions and time-outs

FIVB allows six substitutions per team per set; a starter may leave once and come back once, to the same position, and only for the player who replaced them ([rules 15.1 and 15.6](https://www.fivb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FIVB-Volleyball_Rules2025_2028-EN-v05.pdf#page=38)). NCAA allows 15 per set in Division I and 18 in Divisions II and III, with unlimited entries within that limit ([rule 11.3.2](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/rules/women/PRWVB_RulesBook.pdf#page=51)). NFHS allows 18, and USA Volleyball 15 ([rule 15.1](https://usavolleyball.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2025-2027-USAV-Indoor-Rules-Book_FINAL.pdf#page=52)). The re-entry details outside FIVB vary, so the match tracker only counts substitutions against the limit there.

Every rule set gives each team two time-outs per set. Their length differs: 30 seconds under FIVB and USA Volleyball, 60 seconds in NFHS, 75 seconds by default in NCAA ([rule 11.2](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/volleyball/rules/women/PRWVB_RulesBook.pdf#page=50)).

## Hungarian youth competitions

The Hungarian federation's (MRSZ) 2026–27 notices set no special match rules for the U16 championship ([U16 notice](https://hunvolleyhu.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/U16-Orszagos-Bajnoksag-2026-2027-versenykiiras.pdf)), so the official rules apply. The U14 mini competition is played 4 against 4 on a smaller court with its own rules, including a forced rotation after a run of serves ([U14 notice](https://hunvolleyhu.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/U14-Orszagos-Mini-Bajnoksag-2026-2027-versenykiiras.pdf)), and the U12 kismini 3 against 3; this site's six-player diagrams do not apply to them. Check the notice for your own competition among the [MRSZ documents](https://hunvolley.hu/index.php/dokumentumok/).
